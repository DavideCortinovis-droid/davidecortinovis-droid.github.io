Davide Cortinovis

PhD Student in Cognitive Neuroscience

CIMeC, University of Trento

I am a PhD student in the Object Vision Group at the Centre for Mind/Brain Sciences (CIMeC), University of Trento. I investigate the topographic organization of object categories and dimensions in visual cortex and in artificial neural networks.
